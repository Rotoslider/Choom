Glass Studio

Clips, looks and sequences for the Chooms on the Looking Glass

Loading the Chooms…

Eight frames from first to last. Flags are hints from the automatic checks: background (the black lifts: fog, a glow, a flash), push-in (she grows: the camera moved), color, seam (the loop doesn't close), cut (a new shot). Keys on a card: K keep, D drop, R re-roll, Space play.

Drag a clip into On the glass to add it, out to take it off, or onto another clip to swap them. Hover to play, double-click to watch it large. Changes reach the glass when you build.

Write your own clip

Say what she does, start to finish, in one or two sentences. She must end where she began (the Studio adds the camera line and the ending). Five seconds fail least; a dance or a slow turn may want ten or fifteen.

Planned

Each look is a clean picture of her and the line that describes her in it. Every clip in the look starts and ends on that picture; the subject starts every prompt (say “a plain pure black background”), the keep line follows it in every clip (Genesis: her motes stay on her), and loops finish with the ending.

New look: an outfit, full body or asleep

Klein makes a few versions from one of her pictures; pick one and it becomes a look you can plan clips for. An outfit needs Essentials first, then a dozen or more quiet moments; when she wears it comes from its name, like the glass's other outfits. Full body is for the full-body moves and twirls; asleep is for the sleep clips.

A new Choom on the glass

Start from a picture of her: a render, a portrait, her Choom avatar. Klein cleans it up into a picture of her on black (pick the best of a few), then the Studio makes her still relief so the glass knows her, and her clips come from the library like everyone else's. Her name should match her name in the Choom app, so the glass knows when she talks.
